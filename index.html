About Me

Welcome

My name is Omar Salman, and I am a student-athlete at Illinois Institute of Technology in Chicago. I am studying Business and Information Technology because I want to understand both how technology works and how businesses use technology to solve problems. I enjoy learning about information systems, databases, networking, cybersecurity, and the role that technology plays in improving the way organizations operate.

My college experience has given me the opportunity to develop in several different areas at the same time. Academically, I am building technical and business knowledge. Athletically, I compete in men's basketball at Illinois Tech and have also represented Jordan in international basketball competitions. Outside of the classroom and basketball, I have taken on leadership responsibilities that have helped me improve my communication, organization, and teamwork skills.

Education at Illinois Tech

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business and Information Technology at Illinois Institute of Technology, with an expected graduation date of Spring 2028. I chose this field because business and technology are closely connected in almost every modern organization. Understanding both sides can help me communicate with technical teams while also understanding business goals, processes, costs, and decisions.

My coursework includes information technology and management, data modeling, networking, databases, systems analysis, business operations, finance, and other subjects that support my long-term goals. I am especially interested in courses that teach me how to analyze a problem, understand user needs, organize information, and communicate requirements. These skills are important because technology projects succeed when the people involved understand what the organization actually needs.

Career Interests

My current career goal is to become an IT Business Analyst. I am interested in work that connects business needs with technology. I want to learn how organizations identify problems, improve processes, define requirements, and choose technology solutions that create value. An IT Business Analyst can work between business users and technical professionals, which matches my interest in both sides of the field.

I also have a strong interest in cybersecurity and data protection. Organizations depend on technology and information every day, so I believe people working in IT have an important responsibility to help protect systems and data. As I continue through college, I want to strengthen my understanding of security while also developing my analysis, communication, and business skills.

After graduation, I plan to pursue IT Business Analyst and related roles in Chicago. I am also interested in gaining internship experience before graduation and earning professional certifications that support my career. In the longer term, I may pursue a master's degree in accounting or finance because I am interested in combining technology knowledge with a strong understanding of business and financial operations.

Leadership and Campus Involvement

I serve as the Education Officer for the Illinois Tech chapter of the Association of Arab American Engineers and Architects, or AAAEA. The chapter gives students a way to connect with Arab engineers, architects, information technology professionals, graduates, and other professionals. As Education Officer, I want to help create educational opportunities and activities that connect students with professional development and useful knowledge.

I also serve as Treasurer of Students for Justice in Palestine at Illinois Tech and participate in the Muslim Students Association. These activities have strengthened my ability to work with other students, communicate clearly, stay organized, and contribute to a group with shared goals.

Student Leadership Before College

Before attending Illinois Tech, I attended The New English School in Amman, Jordan. I served as Deputy Head Boy on the student leadership team and was also involved with NESMUN. I served as Head of Registration for NESMUN24 and worked as a Creativity, Activity, and Service leader from 2022 to 2024. These experiences taught me how to organize events, communicate with different groups of people, and take responsibility for work that affects an entire community.

Basketball

Basketball has been one of the most important parts of my development. I play guard for Illinois Tech and have competed internationally for Jordan. Basketball has taught me discipline, patience, preparation, communication, and how to stay focused when things do not go as planned. Playing at a high level has also taught me how important it is to accept coaching, learn from mistakes, and keep working on weaknesses.

Representing Jordan is especially meaningful to me because it allows me to compete while representing my country. My international experience includes the FIBA U18 Asia Cup and the FIBA U19 Basketball World Cup. I also made my senior national team debut in the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Asian Qualifiers. My basketball experiences continue to shape the way I approach school, leadership, and my future career.

My Goals

My main goals are to graduate from Illinois Tech, build a strong career in the intersection of business and technology, continue developing as a basketball player, and become someone who can contribute positively to the organizations and communities around me. I want to continue learning, challenge myself, and use the skills I gain from both basketball and technology to become a dependable professional and teammate.
